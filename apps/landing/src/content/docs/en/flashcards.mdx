Flashcards help you recall ideas from your notes, materials and [study notes](../pages/).

<DocStep number={1} example="flashcards">
<header slot="intro">

## Create a set

Choose the material you want to remember.

</header>

Open **Practice**, choose **Generate practice**, and select **Flashcards**. Under **Sources**, choose up to 20 inputs and add an optional focus. Generation runs through your ChatGPT connection and saves the resulting set.

You can create several sets with different source selections or instructions. The set uses the source content selected at generation time.

</DocStep>

<DocStep number={2} example="flashcards">
<header slot="intro">

## Review

Try to recall the answer before revealing it, then rate your recall.

</header>

Open the saved set. Try to recall the answer before revealing it with **Show answer** or Space. Then rate your recall: **Again**, **Hard**, **Good** or **Easy**. The number keys 1 to 4 select the same ratings after the answer is visible.

When reviewing within a subject, Study loads the cards due for that set. When no cards are due, you can choose **Replay flashcards** to work through the set again.

</DocStep>

<DocStep number={3} example="page">
<header slot="intro">

## Keep the material useful

Revisit the source material when a card needs more context.

</header>

Revise the source material and generate a new set when you want your changes reflected in the cards. Archive sets you no longer use; the archive lets you preview and restore them.

For applying the same ideas in questions, try [practice exercises](../practice/).

</DocStep>
