Study writes flashcards from a whole project's sources and schedules them with spaced repetition. You review the cards that are due, rate how well you remembered each one, and Study decides when to show it again.

## Where cards come from

Each project has one set of flashcards, written on your ChatGPT plan from the project's read sources. Each card cites the passages it rests on, and its **Sources** take you back to the page or moment it came from.

The set lives in the project's **Study material**, and in **Flashcards** in the sidebar. Like the [diagram](../notes-and-diagrams/), it starts with **Make** and later offers **Update** when you want new cards from new material. An update revises the set instead of starting over.

Which kinds of source feed the cards is set in **Settings › Processing**, under **Study material**. Code and spreadsheets are left out by default.

## Reviewing

Choose **Review** in **Flashcards**, **Review flashcards** on a project, or **Review now** on Home. Each card shows its question first.

1. Try to remember the answer, then choose **Show answer**, or press Space or Enter.
2. Rate how it went: **Again**, **Hard**, **Good** or **Easy**. The keys 1 to 4 do the same.

![A card in a review turns over to show its answer, then is rated Good.](clip:flashcard-flip "Show the answer, then rate how well you remembered it.")

The cards you're closest to forgetting come first. When none are left, Study says **Review done. Cards come back when they are due.**

Rate honestly. **Again** brings a card back soon; **Easy** pushes it further away. Scheduling uses FSRS, a spaced repetition method that learns from your ratings how quickly you forget each card.

## How many new cards

New cards are mixed in gradually, so a large set doesn't land on you all at once. **New flashcards a day**, in **Settings › Processing**, sets how many new ones a day's reviews may include.

The **Flashcards** page shows what's coming up, including the cards due in the next 7 days. If a project has an exam date, its review tile counts down to it.

![The Flashcards page: the cards due, reviews this week, the streak, and a tile for each project with its exam countdown.](shot:flashcards "The Flashcards page shows what is due, overall and for each project.")

## Browsing and editing

Open a set to look through all of its cards outside a review. Space turns a card over, and the left and right arrow keys move through the set. **Turn all over** and **Turn all back** flip every card at once.

![The Cell biology set open outside a review, with one card face up and the whole set listed below.](shot:flashcard-deck "Browse a set one card at a time, or all at once.")

You can change the set by hand:

- **Edit card** changes its **Question** or **Answer**
- **Add a card** writes one of your own
- **Delete card** removes one

From [practice](../practice/), **Add to my flashcards** turns a question you missed into a card.

## Using cards elsewhere

**Save for Anki** saves the set as a text file, one card per line with the front and back separated by a tab. Anki and most other flashcard apps can import it.
