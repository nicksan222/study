Practice starts from [Documents](../documents/). You choose what to practise, generate a saved set, and work through it at your own pace.

## Generate a set

Open **Practice** in your subject and choose **Generate practice**. The source picker lists the subject's documents. All are selected initially; leave out any you do not want, and keep the selection to at most 20.

Choose exercises or [flashcards](../flashcards/). Add an optional instruction to focus on a topic or skill. Customize the available question settings, then start generation. This work runs through your ChatGPT connection, and the new material appears in the sidebar when it is ready.

If there are no documents yet, create one first. An unprocessed attachment or unsaved note is not a substitute for a selected document.

## Work through questions

The supported exercise formats include multiple choice, written answers, matching pairs and putting items in order. The chosen generation settings determine what appears in a set.

Start a quiz attempt, answer the questions, and use **Next** to continue. Submitted answers are saved as you go. When you return to an unfinished attempt, you can resume it.

## Review your results

Review saved attempts to see your answers and the available scores, explanations and feedback. If a score is still pending, Study shows that state rather than inventing a result.

Use the linked source documents to revisit the material behind the set. If you discover a problem in your notes, correct the document and generate another set from the revised material.

## Keep useful sets

You can generate more than one set from the same documents, with different instructions or question settings. Archive material when you no longer want it in the active list; the archive lets you preview and restore it.

For another way to rehearse the same ideas, use [flashcards](../flashcards/).
