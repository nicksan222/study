Practice starts from [Pages](../pages/). You choose what to practise, generate a saved set, and work through it at your own pace.

<DocStep number={1} example="quiz">
<header slot="intro">

## Generate a set

Choose your pages and the kind of practice you need.

</header>

Open **Practice** in your subject and choose **Generate practice**. The source picker lists the subject's pages. All are selected initially; leave out any you do not want, and keep the selection to at most 20.

Choose exercises or [flashcards](../flashcards/). Add an optional instruction to focus on a topic or skill. Customize the available question settings, then start generation. This work runs through your ChatGPT connection, and the new material appears in the sidebar when it is ready.

If there are no pages yet, create one first. An unprocessed attachment or unsaved note is not a substitute for a selected page.

</DocStep>

<DocStep number={2} example="quiz">
<header slot="intro">

## Work through questions

Answer each question and use its feedback to check your understanding.

</header>

The supported exercise formats include multiple choice, written answers, matching pairs and putting items in order. The chosen generation settings determine what appears in a set.

Start a quiz attempt, answer the questions, and use **Next** to continue. Submitted answers are saved as you go. When you return to an unfinished attempt, you can resume it.

Answer feedback defaults to **At the end**. In **Settings → Quizzes**, choose **After each answer** to review corrections and explanations before continuing. The preference applies to new attempts; written answers can remain pending while grading runs.

</DocStep>

<DocStep number={3} example="quiz" phase="result">
<header slot="intro">

## Review your results

Look back at your answers to find what needs another pass.

</header>

Review saved attempts to see your answers and the available scores, explanations and feedback. If a score is still pending, Study shows that state rather than inventing a result.

Use the linked source pages to revisit the material behind the set. If you discover a problem in your notes, correct the page and generate another set from the revised material.

</DocStep>

<DocStep number={4} example="quiz" phase="result">
<header slot="intro">

## Keep useful sets

Return to saved sets and practise again when you need to.

</header>

You can generate more than one set from the same pages, with different instructions or question settings. Archive material when you no longer want it in the active list; the archive lets you preview and restore it.

For another way to rehearse the same ideas, use [flashcards](../flashcards/).

</DocStep>
