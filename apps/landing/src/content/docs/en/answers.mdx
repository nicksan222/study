A useful answer should help you understand your own material and let you check its evidence. In a [note](../notes/), use **Ask Study** for an explicit request or choose **Answer** on a saved question.

## Questions and notes

You can mark an entry as a question or as a note. Automatic answers are a workspace preference; you can keep them off and request an answer yourself. You do not need to add an `@study` mention to every question.

Answers run through your ChatGPT connection. A missing connection or failed operation is shown beside the work that needs attention. Keep the question and its selected source material clear before retrying.

## Check the source

When an answer includes citations, use them to inspect the passage it relies on. A citation identifies a source and a location, such as a PDF page, a recording time or a text range. Read the surrounding material, not just the quoted phrase.

If the passage does not support the answer, trust the source and investigate the discrepancy. A citation is a way to check a claim, not a guarantee that the claim is correct.

## Keep revisions in view

Notes, answers and authored pages have version identities. When reviewing an earlier result or comparing changes, check which version you are reading. Your own edits and proposed rewrites should be reviewed before you use the result as study material.

When you are ready to collect what you learned, [turn saved notes into a page](../pages/) and use that page for [practice](../practice/).
