A useful answer should help you understand your own material and let you check its evidence. In a [note](../notes/), choose **Ask about this note** in the notebook toolbar to open its Assistant panel.

<DocStep number={1} example="answer">
<header slot="intro">

## Questions and notes

Ask in the notebook’s Assistant panel while keeping your written notes in view.

</header>

Type your question in the Assistant panel and choose **Send message**. The conversation stays with that notebook.

Answers run through your ChatGPT connection. If the Assistant needs your attention, check the connection or error shown in the panel.

</DocStep>

<DocStep number={2} example="sources">
<header slot="intro">

## Check the source

Follow a citation back to the material behind the answer.

</header>

When an answer includes citations, use them to inspect the passage it relies on. A citation identifies a source and a location, such as a PDF page, a recording time or a text range. Read the surrounding material, not just the quoted phrase.

If the passage does not support the answer, trust the source and investigate the discrepancy. A citation is a way to check a claim, not a guarantee that the claim is correct.

</DocStep>

<DocStep number={3} example="page">
<header slot="intro">

## Keep what you learned

Record the explanation you checked in your working notes.

</header>

Add what you learned to the notebook and verify that your changes have saved.

When you are ready to collect what you learned, [generate study notes](../pages/) or use your saved material directly for [practice](../practice/).

</DocStep>
