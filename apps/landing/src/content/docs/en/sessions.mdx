A session is your notebook for one lecture or one sitting. You write notes, record what you hear, and attach the files that go with it. Every session belongs to a project, and you reach it from that project's page.

## Start a session

Choose **New session** from Home or from a project. The new session offers three ways in:

![A new session, with three cards: Record a lecture, Add files and Ask @study.](shot:new-session "A new session offers three ways to start.")

- **Record a lecture**, transcribed on this computer as you listen
- **Add files**, to bring in slides, PDFs, recordings and more
- **Ask @study**, to start with a question

If you started from Home, **Choose a project** picks where the session goes.

Study names each session from its notes. If the name no longer fits, **Regenerate title from the notes** asks for a new one.

## Write notes

Type in the box at the bottom, which says **Write a note, or type @study to ask**, and press Enter to send. A note stays a note: Study doesn't reply to it. To get an answer, mention **@study** anywhere in the note. Typing `@` opens a small menu; Enter or Tab inserts the mention. How answers are built is on the [answers](../answers/) page.

![Typing @ in the note box opens a menu with @study, and Enter inserts the mention.](clip:study-mention "Type @ and press Enter to ask @study.")

## Attach files

Use **Attach files**, drop files onto the session, or paste them with Ctrl V (Cmd V on macOS). Pasted images come in as files. Each file joins your Library, and Study reads it in the background. See [sources](../sources/) for what it can read.

Every attachment has its own thread. **Reply in thread** keeps notes about that file next to it. A faint line under the file says what has happened to it, such as **Transcribed** or **Read**.

If Study got something wrong, open **File details** and choose **Correct this text**. Search and answers use your correction from then on. Reading the file again replaces it.

## Record a lecture

**Record audio** starts recording in the session you're in. The recording is transcribed on your computer. If the transcription model isn't downloaded yet, the transcription waits in [Activity](../background-work/) until it is. When you're done, choose **Stop and send recording**.

Study saves the recording every second while it runs. If the app closes in the middle, the session shows **Unfinished recording** next time, with **Resume**, **Send** and **Discard**.

Only one session records at a time. A recording has a maximum length, and Study sends it when it gets there.

## Edit and compare versions

Each of your notes and each answer keeps its versions.

- **Edit** changes the text yourself. Esc cancels.
- **AI edit** rewrites it for you, with **Improve**, **Summarize**, or your own words in **Tell the assistant what to change**.
- **Regenerate** asks for a new answer.

When a message has more than one version, **Versions** steps through them; the left and right arrow keys do the same. Earlier versions are kept.
