Open **Settings** from the sidebar, or press Ctrl , (Cmd , on macOS). Settings are in two groups, **General** and **AI**. Changes are saved as you make them.

## General

**Language** switches Study between English and Italiano. A preview shows how menus, file sizes and times will look.

**Appearance** offers **Light**, with bright surfaces for daylight, and **Dark**, with dim surfaces for focused study. **Text size** makes everything larger or smaller, from 75% to 200%. Ctrl + and Ctrl − (Cmd on macOS) do the same from anywhere in the app.

![Settings › Appearance, with the Light and Dark themes and the text size.](shot:settings-appearance "Pick a theme and a text size.")

**Updates** has **Check for updates**. When a new version is available, **Install and restart** installs it and restarts Study; save your work first. If you're recording, finish first. See [install](../install/#updates).

**Processing** sets what Study does with each kind of file:

- a table of every kind of source, with the columns **Reading**, **Tidying**, **Search** and **Study material**, where you turn each step on or off. See [sources](../sources/#changing-what-runs).
- **Skip small talk and logistics before writing**: a fast model leaves out small talk, logistics and noise before study material or practice questions are written, so all of your lectures fit, not a sample.
- **New flashcards a day**: a large new set comes in over several days, so reviews stay manageable. Cards you've already learned still come back when due.

## AI

**Overview** shows every AI feature at a glance: what it's for, where it runs, and whether it's **Ready**, **Needs sign-in** or **Not downloaded**. **Set up** takes you to what's missing.

**Language models** is where you connect your ChatGPT plan.

![Settings › Language models: sign-in with ChatGPT, and the model for quick, everyday and demanding tasks.](shot:settings-models "Sign in with your plan, and choose a model for each kind of task.")

- **Sign in with ChatGPT**, and **Sign out** when you want to disconnect.
- **Manage usage** opens your ChatGPT usage settings in the browser.
- **Test connection** sends one small request to check that everything works.
- Under **Models by task**, three tiers: **Quick tasks** names sessions and leaves out small talk; **Everyday tasks** reads PDFs and pictures, corrects transcripts, answers @study, writes study material and practice questions, and grades answers; **Demanding tasks** draws diagrams. Each tier runs on the model recommended for it unless you pick another.

**Transcription** turns recordings into text on your computer. Here you download the model, about 670 MB, and set **Model copies**, from 1 to 4. Each copy uses about 2 GB of memory and transcribes in parallel. The spoken language is detected on its own.

**This computer** shows what Study found and measured about your computer, such as cores and memory, and **Where models run**: for search and for transcription, whether this computer runs them, or why not. You can **Measure again** after a hardware change. The search model, about 135 MB, is downloaded here.

## Where settings are kept

Settings are stored in the same database as your work, in your data folder. See [privacy](../privacy/#where-your-data-lives).
