Search is part of the sidebar. Press Ctrl K (Cmd K on macOS) to reveal the sidebar when needed and focus its search field. Type a name or words from the material you want to find.

## Know the scope

Within a subject workspace, sidebar search is scoped to that subject's sessions and notebook messages. Global navigation can search across subjects and other searchable saved material.

Some sidebar sections have their own lists and filters. Use the search provided by the section you are in, and check the selected subject before assuming something is missing.

## Open a result

Select a result to open the item it belongs to. A session result opens the session; source and document results open their readers. Practice material and question results retain the relevant saved material version.

Press Esc in the sidebar search field to clear the search. If searching fails, the sidebar shows an error rather than presenting an empty list as a successful search.

## If you cannot find something

Try a distinctive word or the item's title. Confirm that the note was saved, that the source has readable content and that you are searching the right subject. A stored original file does not guarantee that every passage has been read or made searchable.

Search helps you return to saved work. When checking an [answer](../answers/), open its cited evidence directly and read the surrounding material.
