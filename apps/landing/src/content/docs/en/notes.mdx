A note is the place to collect what you are learning before you shape it into something to revise. Keep your notes, questions and attachments together in the subject you are studying.

## Capture first

Open **Notes** and start a note. Write in the composer, then choose **Save note**. Ctrl Enter (Cmd Enter on macOS) saves the note. Save drafts before transforming a note: an unsent note is not part of the saved material.

Attach the files that belong with your notes. Reading status and failures stay beside the affected work, so you can see whether a source is ready. See [sources](../sources/) for the difference between an original file and its readable content.

## Ask when you need help

Use **Ask Study** to ask explicitly. A saved question also has an **Answer** action. You can mark an entry as a question or as a note, and enable automatic answers to questions in workspace settings.

Answers use your ChatGPT connection. If access is missing or work fails, the note shows the problem and the available recovery action. See [answers and citations](../answers/).

## Turn the note into a page

Choose **Transform note**. Select the saved material to include, give the page a title, and add instructions if you have a particular goal.

The available changes include clarity, grammar, structure, removing repetition, summarizing, explaining, expanding and translating. Factual corrections are proposals for you to check. You can also combine several notes into one page.

The transformation uses saved notebook entries and selected sources within its context limit; it is not a promise to include every word of an arbitrarily long note. Save important drafts and review your selection before starting.

## Keep the result useful

Open the result in [Pages](../pages/). Review it, edit it and keep the versions you need. Then choose the pages you want to use to [generate practice](../practice/).

The note remains your original working notebook. The page gives its ideas a shape you can read and revise.
