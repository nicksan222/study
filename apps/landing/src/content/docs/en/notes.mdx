A note is the place to collect what you are learning before you shape it into something to revise. Keep your notes in the subject you are studying and use Materials for original files.

<DocStep number={1} example="note">
<header slot="intro">

## Capture first

Write an idea in your subject’s notebook.

</header>

Open **Notes** and start typing on the page. Study creates the note when you pause and saves subsequent edits automatically. Click the title to rename the note. The status at the bottom of the page shows when your writing has been saved.

Open **Materials** to upload files and review their reading status. See [sources](../sources/) for how to inspect the original file and its readable content.

</DocStep>

<DocStep number={2} example="answer">
<header slot="intro">

## Ask when you need help

Ask about what you are learning from the note.

</header>

Choose **Ask about this note** from the notebook toolbar. Type your question in the Assistant panel.

Answers use your ChatGPT connection. If access is missing or work fails, the note shows the problem and the available recovery action. See [answers and citations](../answers/).

</DocStep>

<DocStep number={3} example="page" phase="result">
<header slot="intro">

## Generate study notes

Choose the saved material you want to turn into study notes.

</header>

Open **Practice**, choose **Generate practice**, then select **Generate study notes**. Include the saved note and any other material you want to use. Add a focus if you want Study to emphasize a particular topic.

</DocStep>

<DocStep number={4} example="quiz">
<header slot="intro">

## Review and practise

Check the generated study notes against the material you selected.

</header>

Open the generated [study notes](../pages/) in **Practice**. Edit them as needed, then select them as a source when you [generate practice](../practice/).

</DocStep>
