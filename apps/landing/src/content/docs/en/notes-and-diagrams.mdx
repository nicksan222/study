import DocStep from '../../../components/DocStep.astro';

Write what you are learning in [Notes](../notes/). When you need a summary or explanation from several sources, generate [study notes](../pages/) in Practice.

<DocStep number={1} example="page">
<header slot="intro">

## Choose the change you need

Revise an idea in your notebook or give the generated study notes a specific focus.

</header>

Edit your working notes directly in the notebook. Study saves the edits automatically.

For generated study notes, use the optional instructions to ask for the form you need, such as a short summary for revision or an explanation of how two ideas connect.

</DocStep>

<DocStep number={2} example="page">
<header slot="intro">

## Combine related material

Combine related material while keeping control of what you include.

</header>

In **Practice**, open **Generate practice** and choose **Generate study notes**. Select the relevant notes, materials or earlier study notes under **Sources**, then explain what you want to combine.

Check that your notebook edits have saved before selecting them. Review the selection before generating.

</DocStep>

<DocStep number={3} example="page" phase="result">
<header slot="intro">

## Review before relying on it

Check the proposed result against the material you trust.

</header>

Read proposed factual corrections against your original sources. Keep important qualifications and disagreements rather than accepting smoother wording as proof of accuracy.

Open the generated study notes in **Practice**. Edit the text directly and use the version controls to revisit earlier text.

</DocStep>

<DocStep number={4} example="quiz">
<header slot="intro">

## Use the revised result

Save the revised material and use it for your next practice session.

</header>

Choose the revised study notes as a source when [generating practice](../practice/) or [flashcards](../flashcards/). Generate a new set when you want it to reflect later edits.

</DocStep>
