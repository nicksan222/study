Capture while you learn; revise when you need a clearer account. [Notes](../notes/) hold the working notes. [Pages](../pages/) give you a place to organize the result.

<DocStep number={1} example="page">
<header slot="intro">

## Choose the change you need

Choose the kind of revision that will make your material more useful.

</header>

A transformation can improve clarity or grammar, organize the structure, remove repetition, summarize, explain, expand or translate. Add your own instructions when the goal is more specific.

A shorter summary is useful for a quick revision. A structured explanation is useful when the connections matter. Choose the operation for the job, rather than asking every transformation to do everything.

</DocStep>

<DocStep number={2} example="page">
<header slot="intro">

## Combine related material

Combine related material while keeping control of what you include.

</header>

Use **From notes** in Pages to bring several saved notes into one page. Select the relevant notes and sources, then explain what should be combined. You can also merge existing pages.

Save unsent notes before starting. Review the selection and the result: long material is subject to the transformation's context limit.

</DocStep>

<DocStep number={3} example="page" phase="result">
<header slot="intro">

## Review before relying on it

Check the proposed result against the material you trust.

</header>

Read proposed factual corrections against your original sources. Keep important qualifications and disagreements rather than accepting smoother wording as proof of accuracy.

Use the editor for your own changes and the version controls to revisit earlier text. Reading an earlier version and making it current are separate actions.

</DocStep>

<DocStep number={4} example="quiz">
<header slot="intro">

## Use the revised result

Save the revised material and use it for your next practice session.

</header>

Choose the resulting page when [generating practice](../practice/) or [flashcards](../flashcards/). The selected version provides the material for that generation. Revise first, then generate a new set when you want your changes included.

</DocStep>
