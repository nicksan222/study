In **Practice**, you can generate editable study notes from saved notes, materials and earlier study notes.

<DocStep number={1} example="note">
<header slot="intro">

## Create from your own material

Choose the sources you want the study notes to cover.

</header>

In **Practice**, open **Generate practice** and choose **Generate study notes**. Under **Sources**, select up to 20 inputs. You can give the result a title and add instructions before choosing **Generate**.

Ask for a summary, an explanation or a synthesis of several sources.

</DocStep>

<DocStep number={2} example="page" phase="result">
<header slot="intro">

## Read and revise

Review the result and keep track of your revisions.

</header>

Open the result under **Saved study notes** in **Practice**. Edit the text directly. Study saves your changes automatically. Use the version controls to review earlier text and **Make current** to restore a version.

Check important claims against the sources you selected.

</DocStep>

<DocStep number={3} example="quiz">
<header slot="intro">

## Practise from study notes

Use the study notes you reviewed to build your next practice set.

</header>

In **Practice**, choose **Generate practice**, select your study notes under **Sources**, then choose exercises or flashcards.

Generation uses the versions selected at the time. After editing the study notes, generate a new set to include your changes.

</DocStep>
